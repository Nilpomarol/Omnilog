# OmniLog — Warm Personal Editorial Style

This is the visual direction for the app. It supersedes earlier dark-first visual guidance; the Home information architecture in `omnilog-home-social-aware-redesign.md` still applies.

OmniLog should feel like a personal collection of stories: warm, tactile, calm, collected, bookish, and refined without becoming formal or magazine-like.

## Foundation

- Ivory background, cream surfaces, warm charcoal text, and warm grey secondary text.
- Muted forest/olive as the primary accent; restrained terracotta, ochre, and dusty blue where useful.
- Artwork supplies the strongest colour. Avoid decorative gradients and excessive colour coding.
- Light is the default for an unset theme preference. Explicit Light, Dark, and System choices remain respected; dark mode uses warm charcoal and softer olive.
- Use bundled Lato Bold for display text, headings, and media titles. Keep Lato Regular for reading text and metadata, with Semibold for supporting emphasis. The stronger sans-serif direction keeps the app warm and personal without the earlier editorial serif treatment.

## Composition

Warm before minimal. Personal before editorial. Artwork before decoration. Whitespace before containers. Typography before borders. One strong action before many equal actions. Different content deserves different layouts. Data should feel like personal history.

Home applies these principles through horizontal Continue cards, a single-row carousel of borderless planned items, one concise rhythm/objectives surface, an activity row, and a small paused shelf. Continue cards use a full-height, edge-aligned cover cropped to fill the artwork area, without text or status overlays. Planned items have no card surface: a 72 by 108 dp cover, top-aligned title and collection details, and a small tonal Començar button beneath them; they omit the media type. Paused items remain an 88 by 132 dp cover shelf. These distinctions preserve the visual priority of Continue cards. Progress changes animate gently.

Keep actual progress, ratings, history, objectives, and personal choices central. Avoid generic greetings, quotes, decorative illustrations, hero banners, and social content without real supporting data.

## Delivery and verification

The shared palette and selective typography are applied app-wide. Home implements the new composition. Existing detail and library layouts can adopt the composition principles incrementally; this change is not a full redesign of every destination.

Device review is still required at normal and 200% font scale, with light/dark themes, long titles, missing artwork, all current types hidden, empty sections, and four objectives. Verify progress-sheet commits/completion, search, Stats, objectives, chronology, and paused-item navigation. Continue cards are Home's hero: they share a fixed 164 dp height at normal text size; larger text increases every card uniformly. Covers are 110 dp wide, and the compact plus control keeps a 48 dp touch target. Goal indicators wrap into two columns for larger text.
